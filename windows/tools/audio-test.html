How to run a useful input test

Record a few seconds of room silence, then speak or play your test source. Stop, select the quiet interval with the From/To sliders, and click “Use selection as noise”. Select the signal interval to inspect its spectrum and estimated SNR. Charts and report use that region and the selected channel; playback and WAV contain the entire recording. The dotted ochre spectrum is the maximum across sampled frames; sage is their power average. Long recordings are sampled at up to 240 evenly spaced FFT windows, so very brief transients may be missed in the spectrum. Peaks and RMS use every selected sample. Browser/device resampling or hardware processing may still occur; capture settings appear in the JSON report. Shared audio availability depends on the browser and chosen surface; enable “Share audio” in the picker. See browser capture details.
